General surgical care

A consultation is a place to review a referral, discuss a surgical concern, or follow up after an operation. Dr. Bashir’s practice also includes trauma and emergency surgery.

Bring your referral and any discharge summary from an earlier admission.

Arrange a consultation

Laparoscopic & gastrointestinal surgery

Dr. Bashir offers laparoscopic, minimal access and gastrointestinal surgical care. If you have been advised to have an operation, the consultation is an opportunity to discuss the proposed approach and its alternatives.

Bring any previous scan images and their written reports.

Arrange a consultation

Hernia & abdominal wall surgery

Patients can consult Dr. Bashir about a hernia or an abdominal wall concern. Discuss what you have noticed, any previous repair, and how the problem affects your daily activities.

If you have had a repair before, bring the operation or discharge notes if available.

Arrange a consultation

Breast & endocrine surgery

Dr. Bashir sees patients referred for breast and endocrine surgical assessment. Bring your questions about the referral, any investigations already completed, and the next steps.

Bring any imaging, laboratory or biopsy reports you already have.

Arrange a consultation
