About Dr. Bashir

Dr. Muhammad Nauman Bashir is a general and laparoscopic surgeon at Hassan Hospital in Toba Tek Singh. He sees patients referred for surgical assessment as well as those returning to discuss their reports or follow-up care.

His route into surgery included a year at Allied Hospital Faisalabad and community medical work in Toba Tek Singh. Today his practice spans gastrointestinal, hernia, breast and endocrine surgery.

Dr. Nauman BashirMBBS, FCPS (Surgery)

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS)
  • Fellowship of the College of Physicians and Surgeons, FCPS (Surgery)

Clinical experience

  • Allied Hospital Faisalabad, 2018–2019. House officer rotations in medicine, surgery, cardiology and neurosurgery.
  • Rural Health Centre, Toba Tek Singh, 2019–2020. Outpatient consultations, community outreach and preventive health work.
  • Basic Health Unit 361 GB, 2020. In-charge medical officer in Toba Tek Singh district.

Current practice

Consultations take place at Hassan Hospital. If you have a referral or previous scans, bring them so you can discuss your particular concern with Dr. Bashir.
